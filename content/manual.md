# How to work with me

A manual for the people I work with, so you do not have to spend three months
working me out. Always evolving.

## Hours

- **Mornings.** I am online from 7am, usually blocked for catching up, crits
  and reviews. I try to stop taking meetings around 3 or 4pm.
- **Lunch.** I block it, or I forget to eat. Dog goes out, messages get caught
  up on.
- **Meetings.** I schedule them five past the hour so there is a gap. Nothing
  breeds burnout like back to back all day. I make coffee the slow way.
- **Evenings.** I may log back in to review work, write, or prep. If you see me
  online, message me. I will flag when I am unavailable.
- **Heads down.** I have ADHD and I am easily distracted. When I need
  uninterrupted focus I set Slack to away, turn off notifications, and block
  the calendar with HDT.

## Communication

- **Slack first.** I am in a lot of meetings, so Slack carries most of it, both
  real time and delayed. I read messages as they arrive to work out whether
  they need a fast answer. If you need one by a certain time, say so. Otherwise
  I will reply as I can, normally by end of day.
- **Huddles.** I would rather hop on a quick call than go back and forth in
  writing. Even if my calendar looks full, I will make time.
- **In the room.** During one to ones, design reviews and team syncs I try to
  stay present and off Slack.
- **Debate is a love language.** My debating style can come across as
  aggressive. I apologise. I do not know what is best, and I use opinions to
  build on a conversation. They are loosely held and sometimes firmly
  delivered.
- **Interruptions.** I interrupt people. I apologise, and I am working on it.
  My real fear is missing a good idea because I or someone else loud did not
  leave room. Speak up, especially in the minority view.

## How I work

- **Curiosity first.** I thrive on discovery and I will ask a lot of questions,
  some of them hard, to get the full context of a problem.
- **Empathy underneath.** Trust and confidence matter to me. I want people
  building relationships across teams, in an environment where contributing
  feels safe.
- **Collaboration.** The good ideas come out of it. Bring other perspectives
  in, show your process, make the thing together.
- **Feedback loops.** I ask for feedback often and build reflection into my
  month. Tell me how my style lands on you. Mine will be direct, will usually
  open with a question rather than a trap, and will focus on why.
- **No gatekeeping.** I try to be open about the work and my intentions. You
  should have the information you need.

## What good looks like

- **Clarity of purpose.** Clear, impactful solutions that meet a real need.
- **High craft.** Detail and quality in execution are non negotiable.
- **A clear why.** A great outcome gets you half the credit. If you cannot
  explain why it worked, we cannot do it again.
- **Empowered teams.** I succeed when the team is thriving, autonomous and
  shipping with confidence.
- **Slipstreams.** Our job is to make it easier for the people behind us. If
  you get traction, pay it back so others can stand on your shoulders.
- **Aligned partnerships.** Strong relationships across functions, on shared
  goals.

## Working best with me

- **Be direct.** Candour is welcome. Concerns, ideas, feedback, all of it.
- **Work out loud.** I know it is hard against a high bar. Share early anyway.
- **Be prepared.** Bring the context behind the idea.
- **Bring your perspective.** I trust your expertise. Argue for your view even
  when it cuts against mine.
- **Stay focused.** Prioritise the high impact work. I will try to do the same.
- **Ask.** If something feels off, lead with curiosity rather than assumption.

## What you can expect from me

- **Big picture.** I will connect dots across projects, teams and goals.
- **Empowerment.** I will trust your judgement. You own your day to day.
- **Support.** Feedback, guidance, or a sounding board. I do not micromanage.
  Ask and I will weigh in. If you do not speak up, I will assume you are clear.
- **Feedback.** Give me at least one piece of constructive feedback a month.
  You will not hurt my feelings, and hard feedback improves the partnership.
- **Vision.** A clear direction, set inside the wider team and company goals.
- **Adaptability.** If it is not working, I am happy to change course.
