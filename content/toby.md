# Toby Barnes

<!-- Edit the bullet text beneath each year. Use one bullet per timeline event. -->

## 1990

- Started a BA in Business Information & Management at Liverpool John Moores University (1990–1994).
- Minors : Economics, Systems Thinking, Information Systems and Marketing.
- Chairman of the Role Playing Society for 4 years. 🤓

## 1992

- Digital Strategy Executive at Dun & Bradstreet (Feb 1992–Feb 1996).
- Hired while at University
- Worked on early internet transformational projects for global brands.

## 1996

- Head of Content Innovation at NTL Interactive (Nov 1996–Dec 1998).
- Launched Europe’s first commercial interactive TV service
- Leading a team of 30 designers and engineers.

## 1998

- Head of Interactive at MTV UK, Northern & Eastern Europe (Dec 1998–Mar 2003).
- Led web, mobile and interactive TV
- Launched BAFTA-winning MTV2.co.uk
- Won many awards for a range of worlds first 360° projects

## 1999

- Met Emily at MTV 😘

## 2003

- Creative Director at Twelve Ten (Oct 2003–Oct 2004).
- Archie was born 👶

## 2004

- Founder and CEO of Pixel-Lab (Apr 2004–Mar 2011).
- Founded Mudlark after acquiring 2 production companies
- Partnering with large and small video-game companies on product and company strategy
- Launched projects including Such Tweet Sorrow and Chromaroma.

## 2005

- Co-founded and directed the original London Games Festival (Mar 2005–Dec 2009).
- Produced games, art, performance and skills events.
- Robin was born. 👶

## 2006

- Got married. 💍

## 2007

- Fraser was born. 👶

## 2009

- Founded Chromaroma (Dec 2009–Dec 2011).
- The Oyster-card social game raised two funding rounds and is now a permanent exhibit at MoMA.

## 2011

- Product Strategy Director at AKQA London (May 2011–Aug 2013).
- Led AKQA’s global Product Design and Business Innovation practices.

## 2013

- Moved from London to Portland as AKQA Product Strategy Director for Nike (Aug 2013–Aug 2016).
- Launched Nike Fuelband, Nike+ Kinect Training, and Nike Training Club
- Strategic Advisor to TrackShift (Sep 2013–Dec 2019), helping musicians get paid faster.

## 2015

- “Swiss Army Knife” at A Strangely Isolated Place (Jan 2015–present).

## 2016

- Group Director for Nike at AKQA (Sep 2016–Jun 2018).
- Led Nike customer-experience work, including Nike Training Club, Nike Membership and the Nike retail App pilot.

## 2017

- Mentor and Advisor to Jaguar Land Rover (Jan 2017–Feb 2018).
- Worked with the first three incubator cohorts.

## 2018

- Global Executive Director at AKQA (Jun 2018–Jun 2019).
- Led AKQA’s Consumer Experience team on work for Nike, Levi’s, Amazon and Beats.

## 2019

- Senior Director, Consumer Experience at Nike (Jun 2019–May 2021).
- Led digital and physical services, experiences and store concepts.
- Opened Nike Flagship in NY, and Shanghai
- Opened Nike live in LA
- Launched Nike app at Retail

## 2020

- Led the Nike Rise retail concept and global launch.
- Opened Nike Rise in Seoul, Madrid, and Chicago

## 2021

- Head of Design for Amazon Alexa (May 2021–Jun 2022).
- Led Alexa’s end-to-end experience, including Hey Disney!

## 2022

- Design Principal for Amazon Alexa+  (Jul 2022–Jun 2023).
- Set the vision and strategy for Alexa’s move into LLMs.

## 2023

- Head of Design, Commerce at Cash App (Jun 2023–May 2025).
- Led design across Cash App, Afterpay and Cash for Business.

## 2024

- Joined the board of Hoyt Arboretum Friends (Mar 2024–present).

## 2025

- Design Director at Shopify (Jun 2025–present).
- Leading product and brand design to help merchants grow their businesses.
